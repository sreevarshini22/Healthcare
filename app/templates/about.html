About MediVault

MediVault was established with a singular objective: to provide individuals and families with a reliable, structured, and private health management portal.

Our Mission

In modern healthcare, patients often consult multiple physicians, diagnostic labs, and specialists across different hospital networks. Medical records frequently become fragmented across patient portals, paper files, and email attachments. MediVault bridges this gap by offering a single, secure repository where patients maintain full ownership of their longitudinal health records.

Patient-Centric Control

Every record is tied directly and exclusively to your account. You decide what to store, update, or permanently delete.

Data Privacy & Security

Documents are kept in private directories inaccessible to unauthorized users or web indexers.

Key Principles

  • Transparency: Clear policies on how your data is handled. We never monetize or distribute personal health information.
  • Accessibility: Fast, clean, and intuitive interfaces that work smoothly on computers, tablets, and smartphones.
  • Data Portability: View documents directly in the browser or download your complete original files whenever needed.
  • No Medical Modification: MediVault preserves your records verbatim and does not alter prescribed medications or clinical dosages.
Notice: MediVault is a personal health record management platform and does not provide clinical diagnosis, medical treatment recommendations, or emergency response services. Always consult licensed medical professionals for health inquiries.
System Specifications
  • Application: MediVault v1.0
  • Supported Files: PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP
  • Max File Upload: 16 MB per document
  • Data Storage: Isolated Directory Vault
  • Security Standard: OWASP / Role-Scoped Access
